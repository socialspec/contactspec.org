# About SocialSpec.org

SocialSpec.org is a collection of APIs for social engagement on the world wide web.

There are currently three APIs:

* https://followingspec.org  

  `/.well-known/following.opml`

* https://aboutspec.org 

  `/.well-known/about.md`

* https://contactspec.org 

  `/.well-known/about.vcf`

And there may be additional APIs added over time.

SocialSpec is also _open source_! 
Follow along at https://github.com/socialspec.
You can help shape the future of SocialSpec by joining one of the RFCs:

* https://github.com/socialspec/socialspec.org/issues/1

* https://github.com/socialspec/followspec.org/issues/1

* https://github.com/socialspec/aboutspec.org/issues/1

* https://github.com/socialspec/contactspec.org/issues/1
